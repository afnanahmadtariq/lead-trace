Insight I Q is now Lead Trace. We listened to feedback from industry professionals and used it to shape a broader direction for our project.

Today, Lead Trace brings prospect research, source evidence, and deal briefs together.

We are building toward lead generation, customer relationship management, and connected sales workflows. Those features are planned, and we will refine them as we progress.

Start with a prospect and describe what you sell. Choose meeting preparation or personalized outreach. The application keeps your prospect, offer, and goal together.

Research runs in the background. The system finds public sources, extracts relevant claims, and builds a brief with the evidence attached.

Each conversation angle connects a source claim to a possible fit for your offer. A question helps you test whether that fit is real. Hypotheses stay separate from facts.

Open a source, check the gaps, and review the outreach draft. Copy a question or download the complete brief for your own workflow. These screens use demonstration data.
