Lead Trace connects lead discovery, a built-in CRM, and evidence-backed sales research in one workspace.

We listened to feedback from industry professionals. Insight I Q is now Lead Trace, expanded around the daily work of sales teams.

Start with your buyer profile. Search companies and people, then inspect the evidence for industry, role, and location.

Deep discovery combines web search, full-page reading, company-site crawls, and public registries. A source-supported match still needs human review.

Qualify a prospect before saving it to CRM. Source quotes stay attached to the contact.

Keep ownership, notes, opportunities, and research together. Each contact highlights the next follow-up.

Use Board or Table view to track deals. Filter follow-ups by overdue, today, or upcoming, and reopen completed actions.

Prepare for a meeting or draft outreach using your prospect and offer context. Inspect source claims, hypotheses, and questions before use.

Discovery and research run in the background. The API and Python worker use stored evidence, with Qwen supporting qualification and briefing.

Connect your own Google Calendar to create a meeting from a contact. Invitations are optional. Two-way Calendar sync and email integration remain planned.

A recorded live audit found nine prospects, with one supported by all requested criteria. The CRM-to-research journey completed. This small cohort is not a coverage benchmark.
