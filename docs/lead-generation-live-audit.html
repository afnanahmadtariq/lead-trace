What changed in discovery

  • Deep mode identifies up to eight sourced companies, including current employers from structured professional experience, then searches their teams and business offerings.
  • Tavily Search is supplemented by Extract and Crawl. Budgets are bounded: 30 searches, 16 extraction URLs, four crawls of up to three pages each, 64 retained source snapshots, and 40 prospects.
  • GLEIF supplies legal-name and reported-address records; Wikidata corroborates sourced company websites; SEC EDGAR checks US public-company identities. Registry facts cannot manufacture people, roles, industry fit, or buying intent.
  • Company domains must be sourced; lead directories are rejected as official company websites.
  • Optional equivalent titles include VP Sales, Sales Director, and Chief Revenue Officer. Sales operations, enablement, junior roles, and assistants do not qualify as senior sales leadership.
  • Industry evidence must describe the company's own offering. A biography mentioning SaaS experience, or selling to SaaS customers, cannot establish that the employer is a SaaS company.
  • Old dates in announcement text are checked even when the provider omits publication metadata. Ended jobs, conflicting current-role fields, and fabricated quotes remain rejected or subject to review.
  • A database-backed background worker continues after leaving the page. Renewable leases recover interrupted work; cancellation prevents late results from overwriting state. Provider outages and partial coverage remain visible.
