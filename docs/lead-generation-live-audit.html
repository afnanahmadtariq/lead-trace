Fixes made from the findings

  • Reject explicit former positions, ended work history, current-role conflicts, unsupported identities and partial names.
  • Match the requested role against the person’s actual role, rather than terms in an SEO title.
  • Retain an exact source quote for every supported criterion, including company evidence from another supplied source when appropriate.
  • Prevent a surname such as Austin, or merely serving customers in a city, from establishing a buyer’s location.
  • Flag older publication dates / dated URLs and conference, interview or award-list sources for current-role review.
  • Show supported matches separately from missing or stale evidence; keep legacy saved candidates subject to review.
  • Preserve evidence through CRM conversion, and prefer current sources when deduplicating equally complete candidates.
  • Fail a production frontend release if its API URL is not the Lead Trace API domain.

These checks operate on bounded search snippets and limited explicit aliases. They do not establish that every source is fresh, authenticate a person’s identity, discover all potential buyers, or verify contact details.
